Why 2 AM?

An introduction to this little corner of mine.

I've always found something interesting about the hours between midnight and morning.

The idea of this website actually came from a song and an album by LOMBA SIHIR called Obrolan Jam 3 Pagi. It made me think about those hours when the world seems to slow down, but life never really stops.

I started wondering about people.

What are they doing at this hour? Are they working, having conversations, making memories, or simply lying awake with thoughts they can't seem to put away?

Somewhere out there, people are living completely different lives at the exact same moment. They have their own stories, struggles, little victories, and things they rarely tell anyone.

And somehow, I find that thought fascinating.

That's where the name jamduapagi came from.

I wanted to create a little space of my own. Somewhere I could share things I find interesting, thoughts that cross my mind, music I enjoy, things I learn, and little pieces of my life that I want to keep.

But I don't want this place to be only about me.

Perhaps, someday, it could become a place where other people can share their thoughts too. Where strangers can exchange stories, discover new things, and have conversations they might not find elsewhere.

I don't really know what this little corner will become.

I'm still figuring things out, just like everyone else.

But for now, I'm happy to start somewhere.

And if you've somehow found your way here, well...

You're still awake?

Welcome to jamduapagi.
